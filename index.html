FAQ

Quick answers.

How often does my pool need cleaning?

In Central Florida most pools do best with weekly service, because heat, rain, pollen and leaves keep working all year. Pools with a screen enclosure can sometimes go longer between visits. We'll recommend what fits yours during the free estimate.

What is included in a cleaning visit?

Skimming, brushing, vacuuming, emptying baskets, a digital water test, chemical balancing and a quick check of the filter and pump. If we spot something that needs repair, we tell you before doing anything.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your pool's size, condition and how often you want service. That's why the estimate is free: we look at your pool and give you a clear price before we start.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can access the pool area, we take care of everything and you can keep your routine.

My pool is already green. Can you fix it?

Yes. A green or cloudy pool usually needs a recovery clean first, then regular service to keep it clear. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll tell you the best plan.

Do you service saltwater pools and do repairs?

Yes. We care for chlorine and saltwater pools, and the same team handles common repairs, so you don't have to hire someone else.

Do you work with homes and businesses?

Both. We serve homeowners, vacation rentals, HOAs and commercial properties across Central Florida.
